STEP 02 · PREPARE PHOTOS

See what the model will read.

Automatic preparation checks each photo separately. You can also compare individual corrections.

Choose a preparation mode, then prepare the photos.

PHOTO PREPARATION

Choose how to read them

Use the arrows under the photo to turn sideways writing upright. The preview changes immediately; press Prepare photos to apply it before OCR.

Automatic preparation checks perspective, small tilt and lighting after your orientation choice, then crops around detected writing. Compare individual steps for the class demo. Always inspect the result; blemish removal is not enabled.

Every option uses the same official mobile detector. Model choice is saved with this draft.
